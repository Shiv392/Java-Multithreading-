⭐⭐⭐ Problem Statement ==================>
Ek buffer hai jiski capacity 1 hai.
Ek Producer hai.
Ek Consumer hai.

Buffer = Empty

Rules:
Producer sirf tab produce karega jab buffer empty ho.
Consumer sirf tab consume karega jab buffer full ho.
Agar wait/notify na ho

# Producer
Buffer Empty
Produce Item 1
Buffer full.
Fir

Produce Item 2 😅 Buffer already full hai.
Problem.

# Consumer
Consumer aaya
Consume
Lekin buffer empty hai.
Ab kya consume karega?
Problem.

Isliye Communication chahiye

Step 1 : 
Initially Buffer = Empty
Consumer pehle aa gaya.Consumer dekhta hai Buffer Empty , Wo bolta hai
Mere paas consume karne ke liye kuch nahi hai. To
wait();

Step 2 : 
Producer aaya. Lock mil gaya. Buffer me item daal diya.

Meaning : Consumer uth ja.
Ab consumer RUNNABLE.

Step 3 : 
Producer synchronized block se bahar.
Lock release.

Consumer consume karta hai.
Buffer = Empty
